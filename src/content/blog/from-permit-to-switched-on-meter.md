## What happens between signing and producing power?

Four steps: the city permits the plan, a crew installs the system, the work is inspected, and the utility swaps the meter and approves the connection. Production starts on the last one, not when the panels go on the roof.

## How long does the permit take?

Phoenix averages 21 days, which is the number the city page uses. The permit is filed on the homeowner's behalf in most residential jobs, and it is the step where a plan review can come back with a comment that has to be answered before the crew is scheduled.

## How long does installation take?

A single day for most houses of the size this site estimates. The crew mounts the rails, sets the panels and photographs every roof penetration, and a twenty-panel system on a straightforward roof is a one-day job. Roofs with tile, steep pitch or a lot of obstructions take longer, and that is a site visit question rather than a schedule promise.

## What does the utility do?

Two things, and both come after the roof work. It reviews the interconnection application, and after the inspection it swaps the meter for one that can measure flow in both directions. Until that meter is in, the system may be installed and not yet producing.

## What actually holds a job up?

Paperwork and inspection, not the roof work. The three reliable delays are a plan review comment, an inspection that finds something the installer has to fix, and an interconnection queue that is longer than the permit. The useful question for an installer is not how long the installation takes, which is a day, but how long the city and the utility take in that particular city, because that is where the weeks go.

## What changes the timeline from one house to the next?

- The city's permit volume, which sets the plan review wait
- The utility's interconnection queue, which is separate from the permit
- Whether the house needs an electrical panel upgrade before the solar work
- The roof itself, since tile and steep pitches take longer than composition shingle
- The inspection schedule, which the crew does not control

## When does the savings clock start?

When the meter is swapped and the utility approves the connection. That is the date to write down, because it is the first day the system produces power the utility credits, and it is the date a homeowner should expect in writing before signing anything.

## Where do these numbers come from?

The 21 days is the average the Phoenix page uses for that city, and the one-day installation is what the crews on that page report for a twenty-panel system. The Department of Energy's homeowner guide walks through the process from the homeowner's side, and the Energy Information Administration's data browser is the public source for utility and state electricity data.
